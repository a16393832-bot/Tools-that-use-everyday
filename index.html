Questions

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no limits. Ads on the page cover the running costs.

Are my photos or text sent to a server?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, so the content stays on your device.

How do I save a QR code or resized image?

Press and hold the picture on a phone, or right-click it on a computer, then choose save image.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The page is built for phone screens first and also works on tablets and computers.
